# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone contributing to **Stalker++**.

## Our Standards
- Respect privacy and legal boundaries: **never** submit real personal data (PII), leaked credentials, or tools designed to bypass authentication/CAPTCHAs.
- Use **100% synthetic personas** in all tests, screenshots, and documentation examples.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive technical feedback.
